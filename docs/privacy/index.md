# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Loopy is designed to keep user data on the user’s device whenever possible. Most data created or imported in Loopy is stored locally. Selected data may also be backed up to iCloud, exported, shared, or sent to external websites and services as described below.

This Privacy Policy explains how Loopy handles data. External websites and services opened through Loopy are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 1. Information Collected by the Developer

Loopy does not require account registration.

The developer does not operate a server to collect or store your learning content, browsing history, recordings, or imported files. Supported iCloud backups are stored through Apple’s services, as described in Section 6.

The current version of Loopy does not use advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated analytics services. The developer does not sell or rent user data.

When you voluntarily contact the developer by email, your email address and the information included in that communication may be used to respond to your inquiry or provide support.

## 2. Data Stored on the Device

Loopy may store the following data locally on your device:

- imported or user-created video, audio, subtitle, text, and related learning files;
- file metadata, playback positions, time spots, learning boards, playback records, and other learning activity;
- saved words and sentences, dictionary and translation history, meanings, examples, tags, notes, and memorization states;
- browser bookmarks, browsing history, saved tabs, downloads, cookies, cache, and other website data managed by WebKit;
- shadowing recordings and related recording metadata;
- OCR results, generated subtitles, translations, speech-recognition results, and waveform-analysis data;
- app settings, preferences, and downloaded model files;
- purchase and subscription access status, trial eligibility, trial-use records, and promotional offer status.

Loopy uses information provided by Apple’s StoreKit to verify Pro access, restore purchases, and determine trial and promotional eligibility. This information may include product and transaction identifiers, purchase and expiration dates, subscription renewal status, offer-code information, refund or revocation status, and original app acquisition information.

Access status may be maintained on your device. Trial-use records may be stored in secure device storage to prevent repeated use of a previously activated trial.

App Store payments, subscription billing, and offer-code redemption are handled by Apple. Loopy does not directly receive or store full payment-card information. These purchase checks do not send your learning content or imported files to the developer.

## 3. Device Permissions and Access

Loopy may request access to the following device features:

- **Microphone:** Used when the user records their voice for shadowing practice.
- **Local Network:** Used to transfer files from a computer or another device to Loopy over the same Wi-Fi network.
- **Photo Library Add Access:** Used to save images selected or created by the user to the Photos app. Loopy does not request unrestricted access to the user’s photo library for this purpose.
- **Files and Documents:** Used to open, import, manage, export, or create backup files selected by the user.

Permissions can be managed through the device’s Settings app where supported. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Loopy does not use the camera in the current version.

## 4. OCR, Translation, and Speech Recognition

Loopy may use Apple platform features, including Vision and Translation, to recognize or translate text from subtitles, selected video frames, web page snapshots, or text selected by the user.

Some language resources may be downloaded or managed by iOS according to Apple’s policies and device settings.

Loopy may also download model files from Hugging Face. Model downloads may send ordinary network request information, such as an IP address, to the download provider.

After the required models have been downloaded, supported speech-recognition, subtitle-generation, and waveform-analysis processing is performed locally on the device. Loopy does not upload the user’s audio, video, or recordings to Hugging Face for this processing.

Generated results are stored locally unless supported data is included in a backup or the user exports, shares, or sends the results to an external service.

## 5. Browser and External Websites

Loopy includes a browser and web lookup features that allow users to open external websites, dictionaries, translation services, search engines, video-expression services, AI services, and custom URLs.

When the user selects an external provider, Loopy may send the selected word, sentence, example, search query, or user-configured prompt to that provider as part of the requested web address or web page interaction.

Examples may include:

- dictionary and translation websites;
- search engines and image search services;
- YouGlish, Yarn, PlayPhrase, and similar services;
- ChatGPT, Gemini, and other websites selected by the user;
- custom websites or URLs added by the user.

Loading a web page, including a preset or restored browser tab, may send ordinary web request information to that website.

External services may receive the user’s IP address, cookies, login state, browser information, search terms, prompts, or other submitted content. They may also display advertisements, use trackers, or store website data according to their own privacy policies.

Loopy does not control the privacy practices, content, security, or availability of external websites.

Loopy may request website icons through Google’s favicon service. When this occurs, the domain or host of the website and ordinary network request information may be sent to Google.

When web media or subtitle features are used, Loopy may request subtitle, caption, media, or related metadata URLs required to provide playback and learning features.

Cookies, cache, login sessions, and other website data may be stored locally by WebKit until the user clears that data, the website removes it, or the app is deleted.

## 6. Backups, iCloud, and Sharing

Loopy supports backup-file export and iCloud backup and restoration for selected app data.

When iCloud is available, supported backup data may be uploaded to private iCloud storage associated with the user’s Apple Account. Automatic backup is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings → Data Management → iCloud Backup. Users can also create, restore, and delete backups from that screen.

Depending on the backup method and supported features, backups may include:

- saved words and sentences;
- dictionary and translation history;
- vocabulary tags and memorization information;
- selected learning and web playback records;
- browser bookmarks and browser history.

Backup metadata, such as the device name, device or installation identifier, and backup date, may also be stored to organize backups and support restoration.

The current backup format does not include original video, audio, or subtitle files, learning boards, shadowing recording files, downloaded models, cookies, or cache.

iCloud backups are saved restore points and do not continuously synchronize all app data between devices. Backup availability depends on iCloud settings, storage, network connectivity, and operating-system conditions.

When users export or share a backup file, they choose its destination through the iOS system interface. Files may be stored in the Files app or another selected app or service.

Data stored in iCloud or another external destination is subject to that provider’s applicable settings and privacy policy.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data is generally retained until the user deletes it, clears the relevant data, or removes the app.

Deleting the app generally removes data stored inside its local container. However, the following may remain:

- files exported to the Files or Photos app;
- iCloud backups and backup files exported outside Loopy;
- copies shared with another app or person;
- trial-use records stored in secure device storage;
- purchase records maintained by Apple;
- account activity or content stored by external websites and services.

Users can turn off automatic iCloud backup and delete existing Loopy iCloud backups from Settings → Data Management → iCloud Backup. Turning off automatic backup does not itself delete existing backups.

Trial-use records may remain after app deletion to prevent a previously used trial from restarting after reinstallation.

Deleting a record or file in Loopy does not necessarily remove earlier backups, exported copies, or copies held by external services. Those copies may need to be deleted separately.

For questions about data handling or deletion of support correspondence, contact the developer using the email address below. The developer cannot directly delete data stored solely on your device or in accounts controlled by you or an external provider.

## 8. Third-Party Services

Depending on the features used, Loopy may interact with Apple platform services, Google services, OpenAI services, Hugging Face, and other external websites.

These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies:

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

Users should review the privacy policy of any external website or service they use through Loopy.

## 9. Security

Loopy is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of user data. Learning data is primarily stored on the device, with supported iCloud backups and external-service interactions described in this policy.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting access to their device, Apple Account, external website accounts, exported files, and backup files.
